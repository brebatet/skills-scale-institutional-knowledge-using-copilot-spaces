# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, establish testing standards, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans for each initiative
- Establish quality standards, acceptance criteria frameworks, and Definition of Done (testing aspects)
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end test suites
- Manage automation and manual testing workflows
- Track and report quality metrics (coverage, defect rates, test pass rates)
- Collaborate with developers and product managers on acceptance criteria
- Coordinate smoke testing and regression testing before releases

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce cycle time from development to release readiness
- Maintain test coverage and automation standards
- Provide early feedback on feature quality and risks

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of acceptance criteria
- Daily standups (test status and blockers)
- QA sign-off in PR reviews and release checklists
- Quality metrics in weekly status reports

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define system architecture, technical strategy, and design patterns. They guide implementation decisions and identify technical risks to ensure scalability, maintainability, and performance.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design for new initiatives
- Establish coding standards, patterns, and best practices
- Review and approve technical designs for complex features
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support technical decision-making
- Ensure alignment with platform standards and dependencies
- Participate in capacity planning and effort estimation

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Ensure system performance and reliability standards are met
- Enable team growth through mentorship and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and design pattern guidance
- Risk registers and technical dependency tracking
- Technical spikes and investigation recommendations

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Platform Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and platform tools. They enable reliable, safe releases and maintain production observability and incident response capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and deployment targets
- Configure monitoring, logging, and alerting for production systems
- Support release operations and incident response
- Ensure security scanning and compliance in CI
- Document deployment procedures and rollback strategies
- Collaborate on pre-release and post-deploy verification

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system reliability and observability
- Reduce deployment risk and incident impact
- Automate operational tasks and reduce manual toil

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment schedule coordination
- CI/CD status and pipeline improvements
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and tooling updates in status reports

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on process adherence. They support the team's ability to execute and improve continuously.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate blockers and impediments
- Coach team on agile practices and process improvement
- Track sprint metrics and report team health
- Coordinate with Project Manager on schedule and dependencies
- Support continuous improvement initiatives
- Maintain visibility into sprint progress and risks

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery cadence
- Maximize team focus and reduce context switching
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Keep communication flowing and blockers visible

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Blocker escalations and impediment tracking
- Retrospective action items and process improvements
- Team health and velocity metrics

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business leaders who champion projects, provide executive alignment, and ensure organizational support. They approve resources, trade-offs, and major decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context, strategic alignment, and authority
- Approve project charter, scope, and resource allocation
- Resolve strategic conflicts and trade-offs
- Champion the project across the organization
- Provide executive-level risk escalation and support
- Approve go/no-go decisions at major gates
- Participate in milestone reviews and acceptance

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and strategic alignment
- Unblock organizational dependencies and conflicts
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and executive support
- Enable team success through resource and authority backing

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and charter reviews
- Milestone reviews and release announcements
- Escalation of business-level risks and decisions
- Monthly stakeholder updates and executive briefings

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience, interaction patterns, and design standards. They ensure features are usable, accessible, and aligned with product vision.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Establish design systems and interaction standards
- Review features for usability and accessibility (WCAG standards)
- Collaborate with product and development on trade-offs
- Support user testing and design validation
- Document design decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Deliver features that are intuitive and meet user needs
- Maintain design consistency and brand standards
- Reduce rework through early design validation
- Enable accessible, inclusive user experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user research findings
- Acceptance criteria and design specifications
- Design system updates and pattern library maintenance
- Usability testing results and iteration recommendations
